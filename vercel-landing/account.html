🔗 How to Sync

To sync your macros from the extension:

  1. Open the Macro-Assistant extension in Chrome
  2. Click the ☁️ Sync button
  3. Sign in with your account email and password
  4. Your macros will automatically sync!

Your macros are securely stored and will sync across all your devices.
