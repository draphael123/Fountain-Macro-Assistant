TL;DR: Macro-Assistant is designed with privacy first. Your macros are stored locally on your device and optionally synced via Chrome's built-in sync. We don't collect, sell, or share your personal data.

Introduction

Macro-Assistant - Macro Assistant ("Macro-Assistant", "we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our Chrome extension.

Information We Collect

Local Data Only: Macro-Assistant stores all your macros, settings, and preferences locally on your device using Chrome's storage API. This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly enable cloud sync.

  • Macros: The shortcuts and expansions you create are stored locally in Chrome's storage.
  • Settings: Your preferences (theme, shortcuts, etc.) are stored locally.
  • Usage Statistics: Optional anonymized usage counts (how many times macros are used) stored locally for your personal dashboard.

Chrome Sync

If you have Chrome sync enabled in your browser, your macros may be synced across your devices via Google's infrastructure. This is handled entirely by Chrome, and we do not have access to this synced data.

Cloud Sync (Optional)

If you choose to create a Macro-Assistant account for cloud sync:

  • Your email address is used for authentication only
  • Your macros are encrypted and stored securely
  • We do not read, analyze, or share your macro content
  • You can delete your account and all data at any time

Permissions

Macro-Assistant requires certain Chrome permissions to function:

  • <all_urls>: Required to enable text expansion on any website you visit. We only monitor text input fields for your shortcuts.
  • storage: To save your macros and settings locally.
  • clipboardRead: Only used when you use the {clipboard} variable.
  • notifications: To show undo notifications after expansions.

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do NOT track your browsing history
  • We do NOT collect the content you type (except for matching your shortcuts)
  • We do NOT sell or share any data with third parties
  • We do NOT use analytics or tracking pixels
  • We do NOT store any data on external servers (unless you opt into cloud sync)

Third-Party Services

The Macro-Assistant website may use:

  • Vercel: For hosting (they may collect basic access logs)
  • Google Fonts: For typography (font files are loaded from Google's servers)

Children's Privacy

Macro-Assistant is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of any material changes by updating the "Last updated" date.
